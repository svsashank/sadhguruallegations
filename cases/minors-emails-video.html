Did a 2025 video show exploitation of girls at Isha?

Editor: Sashank · 2 October 2026

The claim

In February 2025 a YouTube video by Shyam Meera Singh said leaked emails showed minor girls being exploited during brahmacharya initiation at Isha.

What the record shows

News18 reported that the Delhi High Court ordered the video down as based on entirely unverified material. News18 also reported that Perur police in Coimbatore filed a closure report after questioning 61 students at Isha Home School and Isha Samskriti, plus 13 people working at the centre since 2017. None corroborated the allegations, according to that report. Isha quotes a police line that there was no prima facie case and no FIR.

What was not decided

A closure for want of prima facie evidence is not the same as a completed criminal trial ending in acquittal.

Document gap: the police closure PDF and the High Court order are not attached.
