Did Nakkheeran's allegations against Sadhguru and Isha hold up?

Editor: Sashank · 2 October 2026

The claim

For about thirty years the Tamil outlet Nakkheeran has published allegations against Sadhguru and Isha, including exploitation, brainwashing and illegal activity. Other posts often recycle the same claims.

What the record shows

On 19 March 2026 the Delhi High Court, Justice Subramonium Prasad, directed Nakkheeran to take down the material in Isha's 2024 defamation suit and rejected the outlet's attempt to have the suit thrown out at the threshold. Business Standard, LiveLaw and the Times of India reported the order. The Times of India said the court called the videos per se defamatory and found a prima facie element of malice. A later clarification also directed Google to take down 39 short videos and five English-language videos.

What was not decided

This is an interim order in a civil defamation suit, plus dismissal of a rejection application. It is not a criminal conviction, and it does not try every Nakkheeran story from the 1990s.

Document gap: the certified order is not attached. Isha has published a press note PDF.
