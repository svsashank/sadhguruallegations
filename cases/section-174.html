Did seven people die mysteriously at the Isha Yoga Center?

By Sashank, a volunteer with Isha Foundation · 12 October 2026 · Not an official Isha statement

The claim

Headlines after the October 2024 police report said people die mysteriously at the Isha Yoga Center. The number that is quoted is seven.

What the status report said

The figure is from the same 23-page status report the Coimbatore police filed in the Supreme Court during the monks case. K. Kartikeyan, Superintendent of Police, Coimbatore, filed it. The Hindu reported that seven cases had been registered under Section 174 of the old Code of Criminal Procedure, the section for a police inquiry into a suicide or an unnatural death. Of those seven, two were still under investigation because the forensic lab report had not come. The News Minute, reporting the same filing, said the other five were closed because the investigation found the deaths were not suspicious.

Section 174 is not a murder charge. It is the inquiry the police open when a death is not obviously natural, so that they can say whether a crime is indicated. A closed Section 174 file, on the News Minute account of the report, is a finding that the death was not suspicious. It is not a conviction, and it is not a certificate that nothing happened on the campus.

What this page does not cover

The same status report listed six missing-person cases. That is a separate page. It also listed a 2021 course complaint and the tribal-land FIR. Those are separate pages. Deaths reported after October 2024, including the June 2026 reports from the center, are not in this count. This page is only the seven Section 174 inquiries in the 15-year report.
