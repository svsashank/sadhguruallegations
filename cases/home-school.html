What did the October 2024 press meet say about Isha Home School?

Editor: Sashank · 2 October 2026 · Not an Isha statement

Who spoke, and when

On 17 October 2024, at the Hyderabad Press Club, Yamini Ragani and her husband Narender spoke to reporters. The Hindu reported them as the parents of a boy who had studied at Isha Home School in Coimbatore. Yamini said she then volunteered at the school as a political science teacher for two years, until June 2024, to gather evidence. The South First named her as a former faculty member and her husband as a former regional committee member for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

The claims, as the papers recorded them

The Hindu's account of what they said has four points. Their son was sexually assaulted by another student. They found his notes about it during the COVID lockdown, including a line that he felt suicidal. Emails to the foundation were answered by WhatsApp calls, which they read as a way to leave no trail. They were told the other boy came from an elite family. They also said students were woken at 3 a.m. for a cold shower and made to meditate topless, that girls were not comfortable with this, and that they had an email chain of two faculty members writing to Sadhguru asking for the practice to stop.

The South First added what Yamini said from her time as a teacher. Her son had been at the school eight years and, she said, abused for three of them. On her first day she saw children verbally abused. The school lacked qualified teachers, did not follow board rules, and had no complaints committee until 9 October 2024, after the matter had reached the Supreme Court. She said emails among administrators about Isha Samskriti described children, called balya brahmacharis, woken at 4 a.m., and one mail described a 3 a.m. cold bath and girls meditating bare-chested.

What the school answered

Isha Home School's statement, posted on 19 October and published as an article on 22 October, does not take these points one by one. It says Yamini volunteered as a teacher from 2022 to 2024, after her son had graduated, and was relieved in 2024 because of complaints against her. It calls every claim unfounded and baseless. It says the school has a no-tolerance rule on safety. It says the Tamil Nadu Commission for Protection of Child Rights had already studied the school and given a clean chit. It says the school will take legal action against her. The clean-chit report is not attached to the statement, and the statement does not quote it.

So the public reply is a denial plus a personnel point, not an answer to the notes, the emails, the 3 a.m. practice, or the complaints committee. Those documents are not on this page either.

A case that is not this one

The same week's police status report included a POCSO case against a doctor with Isha Outreach, accused by the principal of a government school in Semmedu. That is a different school and a different accused. It is not this press meet.

Still missing: the child-rights commission report, and the emails the parents say they hold. Either one would let this page quote the claim against the document, instead of against a blanket denial.
