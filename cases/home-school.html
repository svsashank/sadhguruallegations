What were the October 2024 claims about Isha Home School?

Editor: Sashank · 2 October 2026 · Not an Isha statement

The claim

On 17 October 2024, in the same week as the monks case, a former teacher named Yamini Ragani held a press meet and made allegations about Isha Home School. Those claims were then repeated as if the police had found abuse inside the school.

What the school said

Isha Home School's statement of 22 October 2024 says Ragani had volunteered as a teacher from 2022 to 2024, after her son had graduated, and was relieved in 2024 because of complaints against her. The school called the claims unfounded, said it has a no-tolerance rule on safety, and said the Tamil Nadu Commission for Protection of Child Rights had already studied the school and given a clean chit. It said it would take legal action against her. This page does not have that commission report attached, so the clean chit is the school's account of it.

The POCSO case in the police report is a different school

The Coimbatore police status report filed in the Supreme Court that week did include a POCSO case. The Hindu, The News Minute and the Indian Express all described it the same way. A principal of a government school in Semmedu complained that a doctor, named in The News Minute as Sarvanamoorthy, had sexually assaulted nine girl students. He worked for Isha Outreach and did check-ups at a mobile health centre. He was arrested and denied bail. The Indian Express quoted Isha saying the incident was outside the ashram. It is not a case about Isha Home School.

A later complaint, reported by Scroll, is also separate. Parents of a former student of the Isha school alleged abuse by another student between 2017 and 2019, and said the school discouraged them from going to the police. An FIR was registered at the Perur all-women's station against four administrators and a former student. That file is not the October 2024 press meet, and this page does not have its outcome.

Still missing: the child-rights commission report the school cites, and Ragani's press-meet text, so the claims can be quoted one by one.
