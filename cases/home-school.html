What were the October 2024 claims about Isha Home School?

On 17 October 2024 Yamini Ragani made allegations at a press meet. The school says she taught there from 2022 to 2024, was relieved after complaints, and that the Tamil Nadu Commission for Protection of Child Rights had already given a clean chit. The commission report is not attached.
