Were two women monks held captive at Isha?

Sashank · 2 October 2026 · Not an Isha statement

The claim

A father said his two daughters, earlier named Geetha and Lata, were brainwashed and held at the Isha Yoga Center against their will.

What the record shows

The Hindu reported on 18 October 2024 that a Supreme Court bench headed by Chief Justice D.Y. Chandrachud closed the habeas proceedings. Both women, aged 42 and 39, told the court by video that they were living at the center of their own free will, and had said the same to a Tamil Nadu police team. The Chief Justice told the father he could not control the lives of adult children through a legal suit.

What was decided

LawBeat quoted the bench: habeas jurisdiction is well defined and need not be expanded. The closure does not affect any regulatory compliance Isha still has to meet. An earlier similar claim had been rejected in 2016, on Isha's account, after the women said they were staying of their own will.

What was not decided

This decides the detention claim for these two adults. It is not a criminal trial of every other allegation about the center.

Document gap: the Supreme Court order PDF is not attached. The hearing date in news reports is 18 October 2024. Isha's note also refers to a 3 October stay of the High Court police direction.
