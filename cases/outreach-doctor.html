Did an Isha doctor molest girls at the Yoga Center?

By Sashank, a volunteer with Isha Foundation · 6 October 2026 · Not an official Isha statement

The claim

The claim that travels is that a doctor inside Isha molested schoolgirls, and that this shows the Yoga Center itself is unsafe. In the Madras High Court on 30 September 2024, the father in the monks case said a POCSO case had been registered against a doctor working in the same institution, and that the allegation was molestation of 12 girls at an Adivasi government school.

What the police registered

The Hindu reported on 8 September 2024 that Coimbatore district police had arrested S. Saravanamoorthy, who worked on the outreach mobile medical service in rural Coimbatore. The complaint was from the headmistress of a government school. More than five students told her that, at a medical camp held at the school, he had touched them. The Child Welfare Committee inquired and alerted the police. A case was registered at the Perur All Women Police Station under sections 9(e) and 10 of the POCSO Act, which cover sexual assault by hospital staff on a child. He was arrested and sent to judicial remand. The News Minute put the camp at a government school in Thondamuthur, under the Action for Rural Rejuvenation programme, and put the number of girls at 12, in classes 6 to 10. The two reports do not agree on his age.

Isha's statement that week said a case had been registered against an employee who was a doctor with the outreach mobile medical services, that it had a zero-tolerance policy for crimes against women and children, and that it was cooperating with the investigation.

What the later court papers say

The Indian Express, reporting the Coimbatore police status report filed in the Supreme Court in October 2024, said a school principal had lodged a complaint against a doctor employed by Isha Outreach, that he was arrested and denied bail, and that the investigation was still active. Isha's reading of that report is that the man worked for Isha Outreach and that the incident was outside the ashram. The news accounts of the arrest support the second point. The camp was at a government school. They do not place it at the Yoga Center.

This page does not have a judgment. An arrest and a remand are not a conviction.
