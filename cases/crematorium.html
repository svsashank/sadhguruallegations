Is there a secret crematorium at the Isha Yoga Center?

Editor: Sashank · 2 October 2026 · Not an Isha statement

The claim

The version that circulates is not that Isha runs crematoriums. It is that a crematorium inside the Yoga Center is used to burn people who have disappeared.

What Isha says is on the campus

Isha says no crematorium operates on the Yoga Center premises. Since 2010 it has managed government crematoriums in towns and cities, designated by the Tamil Nadu government. In 2020, during COVID, it ran a free service it called Dignity in Death at those sites. News18 and India TV reported on 9 December 2025 that the municipal administration minister, K.N. Nehru, inaugurated a free service for below-poverty-line families at Isha-managed gas crematoriums. Those reports put the number at 17, with three more planned, and quote the minister as saying the sites, called Isha Kayantha Sthanam, had handled over 100,000 cremations. Named locations include Chennai, Vellore, Thanjavur, and in Coimbatore district Nanjundapuram, Veerakeralam, Thudiyalur, Podanur, Vellalore, Alandurai, Thondamuthur, Karamadai and Goundampalayam. Alandurai and Thondamuthur are near the center. They are listed as separate government facilities, not as a hall inside the ashram.

The court case is a different claim

On 12 June 2024 DT Next reported that a Madras High Court bench of Justices S.S. Sundar and N. Senthil Kumar restrained Isha from operating a crematorium at Ikkarai Boluvampatti until the petition was decided, and asked the pollution control board to inspect. The petition said the structure violated the rules. Isha told the court it runs 14 crematoriums in the state, including Adyar in Chennai, and that the Ikkarai Boluvampatti unit was gas-fired with a 100-metre chimney. This page does not have the later order. A restraint on one unit at the village is not a finding that bodies from the ashram were burnt in secret.

Still missing: the designation orders for the government crematoriums, and the final order in the Ikkarai Boluvampatti petition.
