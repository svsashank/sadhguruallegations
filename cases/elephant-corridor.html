Is the Isha Yoga Center built in an elephant corridor?

Isha says the center is not in a notified corridor in the Wildlife Trust of India lists of 2005 and 2017, nor in the 2010 Gajah report, and that the forest department said so in CFCIT/07/2013 and a 2021 RTI reply. The RTI is not attached.
