Satyameva jayate.

This file launched on 2 October 2026, Gandhi Jayanti. The date was chosen on purpose. Truth alone triumphs is the line the republic already uses. It is the only line this site needs.

The allegations against Sadhguru and Isha Foundation are repeated until they sound settled. Many of them have a court order, a police report, or a government paper sitting under them. Those papers are scattered. This is a volunteer fact check that puts the claim and the paper on the same page.

It is written by Sashank. It is not an Isha publication, not a commission, and not a court. Where a document is missing, the page says so. Where a proceeding did not decide the thing the headline claims, the page says that too.

Corrections, documents and missing orders can be sent to sadhguruallegations@gmail.com.
