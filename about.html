Satyameva jayate.

Truth alone should triumph. That is the whole reason for this site. The allegations against Sadhguru and Isha Foundation are repeated until they sound like settled fact. Some of them have a court order under them. Some have a police report. Some have a government paper. Those papers are scattered, and the claim travels faster than the paper. I am putting them on the same page.

I started this on 2 October 2026, Gandhi Jayanti. The date was not an accident. The line the day already carries is the line I wanted here.

The work is one allegation at a time. I write the claim the way it is told, then what the record actually says. Where I only have Isha's account of a document, I say so. Where a hearing did not decide what the headline claims, I say that too. Where an order is missing, the page asks for it.

My name is Sashank. I volunteer with Isha Foundation. This is my own work, not an official Isha site, and it is not a court. A correction, or an order I have not seen, can go to sadhguruallegations@gmail.com.
